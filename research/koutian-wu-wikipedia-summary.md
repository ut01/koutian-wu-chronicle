## Research
- Integrating explainable AI / machine learning into the Noah-MP land surface model to improve simulation of plant–rock–water interactions.
- Project Administrator, NSF NCAR Allocation Project **UTAA0012** — "Explainable AI for Improving Physics-Based Noah-MP Land Surface Modeling of Plant–Rock–Water Interactions." Allocated significant HPC time on the NSF NCAR Derecho-GPU supercomputer.
- Stated interests: AI for land surface modeling, plant–rock–water interactions, HPC for large-scale geoscience simulation, land–climate interactions.
